Recurring Event Sequences in Instructional Fixed-Wing General Aviation Accidents A Sequence Analysis of National Transportation Safety Board Accident Reports, 2020-2025

Tomás Santos · The Collegiate Aviation Review International, 44(2)

An analysis of 728 U.S. instructional fixed-wing general aviation accidents from 2020–2025 examines how accident events unfold. Recurring patterns around landings, go-arounds and power-loss events suggest areas for further research and review of flight-training practices.
